Everything You Need

Built for the Real Challenges of Running a Petrol Station

From the nozzle to the owner's phone — every rupee tracked, every litre accounted for.

⚡
RFID Shift Tracking
Each attendant taps their RFID card to activate their nozzle. Every dispense event is captured automatically — no manual entry, no leakage.
💰
Blind-Drop Reconciliation
Attendants submit cash without seeing expected totals. Variances over ₹50 trigger instant WhatsApp/SMS alerts to the owner and manager.
🏢
Corporate Credit Sales
Manage company accounts with credit limits. Biometric driver verification, FASTag reading, per-fill limits, and automated monthly statements.
📊
Live Owner Dashboard
Real-time sales by fuel type, payment mode, tank levels, attendance, and shift variances — all visible from your phone, anywhere.
⛽
Dipstick & Stock Management
Record opening/closing dip readings with density and temperature. Track shrinkage, deliveries, and get alerts when tank levels fall low.
👥
Attendance & HR
Mark daily check-in/out per shift, manage leave, and get a complete attendance roster. Quick-mark for managers right from the dashboard.
🌐
6 Indian Languages
Full support for English, हिन्दी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ, and मराठी. Attendants use the app in their own language.
📱
WhatsApp Alerts
Instant notifications for cash variance, low stock, credit limit breach — delivered to the owner's WhatsApp and SMS in real time.
📈
Reports & CSV Export
Date-range reports with fuel-wise, payment-wise breakdowns. Export to CSV for your accountant or oil company audits.
Simple to Deploy

Up and Running in One Day

No complex hardware. No long IT projects. Pumpini works with your existing setup.

1
Register Your Station
Add your station, tanks, nozzles, fuel prices, and staff users in minutes from the settings page.
2
Issue RFID Tags
Assign one RFID card to each attendant. Tap to assign to a nozzle at shift start — that's it.
3
Track in Real Time
Every sale, every litre, every rupee appears live on the manager dashboard and your phone.
4
Close & Reconcile
Attendant submits blind cash drop. System computes variance and alerts you instantly if anything's off.
